Why these three: A groups the flat seven-item list into four scannable sections and softens the active state from a flat highlight to a tinted rounded chip — the safest, most broadly "enterprise banking" upgrade. B is the same structure in a dark navy rail for a more premium, differentiated feel against the light content area. C keeps the current density but adds pinned favorites and a minimal accent bar for power users who live in the menu. Say which one to carry across all 92 pages — or mix pieces, e.g. A's grouping with C's favorites row.
